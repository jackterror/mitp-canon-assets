# Two-Faced Pale Spike Monster – Character Lock

## Canon Name
Two-Faced Pale Spike Monster

## Canon Number
8

## Keeper Status
Keeper with special continuity lock.

## Non-Negotiable Face Rules
- Top face stays happy / smiling.
- Lower stomach face appears only when the stomach front is visible.
- True back views show no faces.
- Arms must not cover the lower face in front-facing canon views.
- Side and 3/4 views must respect body rotation.
- Do not invent extra faces.

## Body Rules
- Pale body.
- Blue spikes on top.
- Rounded body shape.
- All-over subtle spiral / circle texture.
- Small arms and feet stay secondary.

## Drift Risks
- Lower face disappears in front view.
- Lower face appears incorrectly in back view.
- Top face becomes angry.
- Arms cover lower face.
- Body becomes too tall or too thin.

## Prompt Reminder
When generating this character, explicitly state that the top face is happy and smiling, the lower stomach face is only visible on front-facing body views, and true back views show no faces.
