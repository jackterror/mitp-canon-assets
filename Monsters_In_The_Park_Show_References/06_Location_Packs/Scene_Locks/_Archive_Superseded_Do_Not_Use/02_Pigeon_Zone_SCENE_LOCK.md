# Pigeon Zone – Scene Lock

## Canon Name
Pigeon Zone

## Canon Number
2

## Purpose
Recurring fountain plaza used for feeding pigeons, light chaos, walk-and-talk beats, reaction shots, background business, and playful movement around a central landmark.

## Core Layout
- The scene is a circular fountain plaza.
- A round stone fountain sits at the center and acts as the anchor prop.
- Concentric or gently radiating cobblestone rings spread out from the fountain.
- A curved rear fence follows the back arc of the plaza.
- A bench and lamp post sit on the left side.
- A matching bench and lamp post sit on the right side.
- Tree and shrub masses frame both sides and the rear edge.
- The skyline sits softly behind the trees and fence.
- Pigeons are distributed loosely across the plaza and near the fountain.
- The layout should feel balanced, open, circular, and readable.

## Fixed Prop Positions

### Center
- Fountain is the main anchor prop.
- Fountain sits centered in the plaza.
- Fountain must remain centered across all views.
- Fountain has a circular basin and stacked tiered center.
- Open paving remains visible all around the fountain.

### Left Side
- Left bench sits on the left side of the plaza, set back from the fountain edge.
- Left bench faces inward toward the fountain / central plaza.
- Left lamp post sits to the outside-left side of the left bench.
- Left bench and left lamp post must remain congruent between front elevation, top-down plan, and performance-zone view.
- Left tree and shrub mass frame the far-left edge.
- Rear fence continues behind the left bench zone.

### Right Side
- Right bench sits on the right side of the plaza, set back from the fountain edge.
- Right bench faces inward toward the fountain / central plaza.
- Right lamp post sits to the outside-right side of the right bench.
- Right bench and right lamp post must remain congruent between front elevation, top-down plan, and performance-zone view.
- Right shrubs and trees frame the far-right edge.
- Rear fence continues behind the right bench zone.

### Rear Arc
- Rear fence line is a hard continuity element.
- Rear tree and shrub masses form a soft semicircle behind the plaza.
- Skyline remains soft and secondary.
- Do not flatten the circular plaza into a straight path or oval that breaks the symmetry.

### Ambient Recurring Elements
- Pigeons are recurring environmental elements.
- Pigeons can shift position slightly from shot to shot, but should remain present in the location language.
- Use a light scattering of pigeons across the open paving and near the fountain.
- Do not cluster all pigeons into one corner unless story action requires it.

## Relative Measurements
Use these as production consistency estimates unless later replaced by measured artboard data.

- Fountain basin diameter: approx. 12 ft
- Plaza width at foreground: approx. 24 ft
- Left bench to left lamp post: approx. 3 ft
- Right bench to right lamp post: approx. 3 ft
- Bench setback from circular curb / plaza edge: approx. 2 ft
- Fountain to rear fence: approx. 10 ft
- Fountain edge to each bench: approx. 8 ft
- Distance between left and right benches: approx. 22 ft
- Circular playable plaza diameter: approx. 28–32 ft

## Measurement Method
- Fountain basin diameter is measured from outer basin edge to outer basin edge.
- Plaza width at foreground is measured from inside curb edge to inside curb edge across the playable paving.
- Bench to lamp post distance is measured from the nearest outside bench edge to the center of the lamp post base.
- Bench setback is measured from the rear bench edge to the nearest inside curb / plaza edge.
- Fountain to rear fence is measured from the rear outer basin edge to the nearest inside edge of the rear fence arc.
- Fountain edge to each bench is measured from the outer fountain basin edge to the nearest front bench edge.
- Distance between benches is measured from bench center to bench center.
- Use ft and in notation.
- Use “approx.” when the value is visually inferred rather than physically measured.

## Camera Views

### Wide Plate
- Shows the full fountain plaza, both benches, both lamp posts, rear fence, surrounding shrubs, trees, skyline, and pigeons.
- Use as the master layout reference.
- Keep fountain centered and benches balanced on both sides.

### Top-Down Plan
- Shows the same circular layout from an angled top-down view.
- Left bench must keep the left lamp post on the outside-left side of the bench.
- Right bench must keep the right lamp post on the outside-right side of the bench.
- Keep the fountain centered and the plaza geometry circular.

### Close / Performance View
- Pushes in toward the fountain foreground and benches without moving props.
- Fountain remains the dominant central anchor.
- Benches and lamp posts may crop naturally, but must not relocate.
- Pigeons can occupy the foreground and circulation paths.

## Character Performance Zones

### A – Left Bench Interaction Area
Near the left bench for seated conversation, reactions, quiet beats, or character waiting.

### B – Main Fountain Foreground Action Zone
Open paved area in front of the fountain for primary staging, group action, and clear readable performance.

### C – Right Bench Interaction Zone
Near the right bench for side conversations, reactions, or secondary staging.

### D – Fountain Perimeter Circulation Zone
Around the fountain edge for walk-and-talk beats, crossing action, and circular movement.

### E – Pigeon Chaos / Eye-Line Zone
Loose crossing space where pigeons and character movement add life and guide the eye through the plaza.

## Do Not Move
- Fountain
- Left bench
- Right bench
- Left lamp post
- Right lamp post
- Rear fence arc
- Circular curb geometry
- Tree masses
- Main shrub masses

## Style Lock
- Muted watercolor
- Dusty sage greens
- Warm beige parchment
- Soft ink lines
- Low contrast
- Light transparent foliage
- Gentle storybook texture
- No high saturation
- No hard poster-like outlines
- No glossy rendering

## Generation Rules
- Do not redesign the layout.
- Do not move the fountain off center.
- Do not swap the lamp post sides relative to the benches.
- Do not remove one bench and keep the other unless a crop naturally excludes it.
- Do not add a trash can to this location.
- Do not turn the circular plaza into a path-based layout.
- Keep rear fence curvature consistent.
- Push in by cropping from the approved wide plate, not by rebuilding the scene.
- Keep pigeons present as a recurring location cue, but allow small position changes.
- Characters occupy the open plaza and bench zones, not the locked prop footprints.
