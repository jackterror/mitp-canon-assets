# Concession Stand Building – Scene Lock

## Canon Name
Concession Stand Building

## Canon Number
3

## Keeper Status
This location technical sheet is locked as the keeper.

Keeper image:

Use this sheet as the visual authority for the Concession Stand Building layout, prop placement, performance zones, and technical-sheet styling.

## Purpose
Recurring treat-stop and social anchor where monsters gather for snacks, quick interactions, casual meetups, short breaks, playful bits, and service-window business.

## Core Layout
- The scene is a wide open cobblestone plaza with the concession stand building anchored on the right side.
- Most playable action happens in the open space to the left of the building.
- Two benches sit on the left side of the scene.
- A single lamp post stands between the left bench cluster and the center of the plaza.
- The concession stand building sits on the right with the service window facing front.
- A trash can sits between the open plaza and the building, slightly left of the building mass.
- Queue stanchions sit in front of the building and define the service approach area.
- A curved fence and shrub line run across the rear of the location.
- Trees and a soft skyline sit behind the fence.
- The scene should feel open, readable, warm, and built for character business.

## Fixed Prop Positions

### Concession Stand Building
- Building is the dominant right-side anchor.
- Building sits right of center, clearly separated from the main left-side action space.
- Front service window faces camera in the front elevation.
- Awning, sign, menu board, and front service opening must stay consistent.
- Do not slide the building toward center.
- Do not rotate the building away from the service-window-facing orientation in the front elevation.

### Bench Cluster
- Two benches sit on the left side of the plaza.
- Benches are arranged with the lamp post between them.
- Left bench sits farther left.
- Right bench sits slightly right of the lamp post but still part of the left-side cluster.
- Benches face camera / foreground in the front elevation.
- Benches are the main secondary interaction zone.
- Do not move the bench cluster into the middle of the open plaza.

### Lamp Post
- Lamp post sits in the bench cluster on the left side.
- Lamp post is visually between the two benches in the front elevation.
- In the top-down plan, lamp post stays centered within the bench grouping.
- Do not move the lamp post toward the building.

### Trash Can
- Trash can sits between the open action area and the building.
- Trash can is near the building edge, slightly left of the building mass.
- It supports quick gags and side business near the building.
- Do not move the trash can into the bench cluster.

### Queue Stanchions
- Queue stanchions sit in front of the concession stand window.
- They define the service approach / queue lane.
- They remain clearly on the building side of the composition.
- Do not move stanchions into the center meeting zone.

### Fence Line and Rear Edge
- Rear fence runs across the back of the scene in a gentle curve.
- Rear shrub masses sit directly behind the fence.
- Trees rise behind the shrubs.
- Skyline remains soft and secondary.
- Do not flatten the fence into a dead-straight line.
- Do not break the rear edge continuity between views.

### Cobblestone Ground
- Cobblestone paving fills the playable ground.
- Open plaza area left of the building is intentionally wide and clear.
- This open space is the main meeting / action zone.
- Do not clutter the center-left plaza with extra props.

## Relative Measurements
Use these as production consistency estimates unless later replaced by measured artboard data.

- Building footprint: approx. 12 ft x 10 ft
- Service window width: approx. 6 ft
- Open plaza left of building: approx. 18 ft to 22 ft
- Bench cluster to lamp post: approx. 3 ft to 4 ft
- Trash can to building corner: approx. 3 ft
- Queue depth: approx. 4 ft to 5 ft
- Depth to back fence: approx. 18 ft to 20 ft
- Bench width: approx. 6 ft
- Lamp post height: approx. 9 ft to 10 ft
- Bench-to-bench spread: approx. 8 ft to 10 ft across cluster

## Measurement Method
- Building footprint is measured across the visible base width and estimated side depth of the concession building.
- Service window width is measured opening edge to opening edge.
- Open plaza left of building is measured from the left building edge to the left-side playable curb/fence side across the main action field.
- Bench cluster to lamp post distance is measured from the nearest bench edge to the center of the lamp post base.
- Trash can to building corner is measured from trash can center to the nearest front building corner.
- Queue depth is measured from the building service edge out to the front queue stanchion line.
- Depth to back fence is measured from the foreground action area to the rear fence line.
- Bench width is measured from outside edge to outside edge.
- Lamp post height is estimated from ground/base to top cap.
- Use ft and in notation.
- Use “approx.” when visually inferred.

## Camera Views

### Front Elevation / Camera View
- Main locked camera-facing view.
- Shows two benches and lamp post on the left.
- Shows the building on the right with the service window facing front.
- Shows trash can between the open plaza and the building.
- Shows queue stanchions in front of the building.
- Keeps the center-left plaza open for staging.

### Top-Down Plan
- Uses the same wide-shot logic, translated into an angled top-down view.
- Bench cluster remains on the left.
- Lamp post stays aligned with the bench cluster.
- Building footprint stays on the right and must read clearly.
- Trash can remains between plaza and building.
- Queue stanchions remain in front of the building.
- Rear fence and shrubs must remain congruent with the front elevation.
- Do not invent extra paths or side corridors.

### Close / Performance View
- Pushes in from the approved wide plate.
- One alternate focus is the bench cluster to the left.
- The other alternate focus is the concession stand service window.
- The main action emphasis remains the open area left of the building.
- Cropping may remove props naturally, but must not relocate them.

- Do not crowd the center-left open plaza with extra props.
- Do not move the bench cluster away from the left side.
- Do not move the lamp post out of the bench cluster.
