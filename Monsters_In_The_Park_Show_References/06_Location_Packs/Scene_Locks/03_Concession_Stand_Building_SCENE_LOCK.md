# Concession Stand Building – Scene Lock

## Canon Name
Concession Stand Building

## Canon Number
3

## Keeper Status
Approved keeper.
Use the final Concession Stand Building technical sheet as the visual source of truth for layout, prop relationships, zone logic, and styling.

## Purpose
Cheerful snack and meetup location used for purchases, casual interactions, light queue action, side conversations, and short reaction beats.

## Core Layout
- The scene is a wide paved plaza with most usable action space to the left of the building.
- A concession stand building anchors the right side of the layout.
- Two benches sit on the left side with a lamp post between them.
- A trash can sits between the bench cluster and the concession building.
- Queue stanchions sit in front of the service window.
- Fence line and shrub masses run along the back edge.
- Trees and skyline sit softly in the distance.
- The open plaza in front of and left of the building remains clear for staging.

## Fixed Prop Positions

### Concession Stand Building
- Building anchors the right side of the scene.
- Service window faces camera.
- Awning, sign, menu board, and building face remain consistent.
- Building should not slide left or right between views.

### Bench Cluster
- Two benches sit to the left of the building.
- Lamp post sits between the two benches.
- Bench cluster remains left of the building in all views.
- Benches face camera / plaza.

### Lamp Post
- Lamp post is centered between the two benches.
- Lamp post is a hard continuity element.
- It should not drift toward the building.

### Trash Can
- Trash can sits between the bench cluster and the building.
- Trash can remains near the open plaza edge, not tucked into the fence line.

### Queue Stanchions
- Stanchions sit in front of the service window.
- They define a small service approach / queue space.
- They should not become a large maze or wraparound line.

### Fence Line and Rear Edge
- Rear fence line and shrub masses form the background edge.
- Keep them soft and secondary.
- Fence should not compete with the building as the main anchor.

### Cobblestone Ground
- Open paved ground is a major staging feature.
- Most action happens in the left and center open space.
- Do not crowd the foreground with extra props.

## Relative Measurements
Use these as production consistency estimates unless later replaced by measured artboard data.

- Distance between left bench and lamp post: approx. 3 ft
- Distance between right bench and lamp post: approx. 3 ft
- Bench cluster to building: approx. 18–20 ft
- Trash can to building edge: approx. 4–5 ft
- Trash can to lamp post: approx. 8–10 ft
- Building frontage width: approx. 16–18 ft
- Building depth: approx. 8–10 ft
- Open staging plaza width: approx. 24–28 ft
- Queue stanchion depth: approx. 3–4 ft

## Measurement Method
- Bench to lamp post distance is measured from nearest bench edge to lamp post base center.
- Bench cluster to building is measured from the right bench outer edge to the nearest building wall edge.
- Trash can distance is measured from its base center to the nearest building or lamp-post reference point.
- Building frontage width is measured across the main front face.
- Open staging plaza width is measured across the usable clear area left of the building.
- Use ft and in notation.
- Use "approx." when visually inferred.

## Camera Views

### Front Elevation / Camera View
- Uses the approved wide shot as the master.
- Building sits on the right.
- Bench cluster and lamp post sit on the left.
- Trash can stays between them.
- Open plaza remains readable.

### Top-Down Plan
- Must align with the wide shot.
- Building remains the right-side anchor.
- Bench cluster remains left.
- Trash can remains between the two zones.
- Queue stanchions stay in front of the service window.

### Close / Performance View
- Main close action can happen in the left plaza, the bench area, or at the concession window.
- Push in from the wide shot rather than rebuilding.
- Preserve the building-to-bench relationship.

## Character Performance Zones

### A – Attractor Character Interaction Area
Concession stand service window and immediate building face.

### B – Main Meeting / Space Action Zone
Open plaza left of the building for primary staging.

### C – Left Side Interaction Zone
Bench cluster and lamp post area for side conversations and seated beats.

### D – Building Edge / Trash Can Gag Zone
Area near the building edge and trash can for short interactions or prop gags.

### E – Service Approach / Queue Zone
Immediate service approach in front of the stanchions and window.

## Do Not Move
- Concession stand building
- Building sign / awning / menu face
- Left bench
- Right bench
- Lamp post
- Trash can
- Queue stanchions
- Fence line
- Main open plaza footprint
- Tree and skyline depth

## Camera Can Push In Here
- Left bench area
- Open center-left plaza
- Concession stand window
- Building edge near trash can
- Service approach zone

## Style Lock
- Muted watercolor
- Dusty sage greens
- Warm beige parchment
- Soft ink lines
- Low contrast
- Light transparent foliage
- Gentle storybook texture
- No high saturation
- No hard poster-like outlines
- No glossy rendering

## Generation Rules
- Use the approved wide shot as the base.
- Do not redesign the building.
- Do not move the bench cluster away from the left side.
- Do not move the trash can out of the center-right relationship.
- Do not turn the queue stanchions into a complex queue maze.
- Keep most playable action space left of the building.
- Push in by cropping from the approved wide plate, not by rebuilding the scene.
- Preserve the relaxed, readable plaza layout.

## Notes From Locking Process
- This location worked best once the top-down view clearly followed the wide shot.
- Most action is intentionally to the left of the building.
- Best alternate close views are the bench cluster and the concession window.
